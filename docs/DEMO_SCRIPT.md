# 3.5-minute demo script

Click **Guided demo** in the sidebar; it drives these steps.

1. **Command Centre (20 s).** "SIH26009 is two problems. Satellites can't see ore underground: they narrow where to drill, and they drive the production-risk forecast." Point at the KPI row: September 2026, 4 of 10 mines at risk, deficit covered by the action plan.
2. **Reserve map (60 s).** Fused-confidence layer → switch to *Uncertainty*. "We show where we don't know." Click DT-01: kriged grade ± sd, borehole support, surface-proxy SHAP. Point out spatial-CV AUC vs random-CV AUC, and kriging's two-scale validation. Switch the satellite image to *Sentinel-2 false colour*: "this is real imagery." Scroll to the real-data check: "on real data and the 10 real mine locations the model scores 0.56, which is chance, and bare ground alone scores 0.64. We show it because it's true; GSI occurrence data is the fix." Point at the drill-target tonnage ranges and P(ore).
3. **Forecast — Balaghat (40 s).** P10/P50/P90 fan, 1% chance of the September target. SHAP: the scheduled hoist overhaul and the monsoon outlook. The target is above even P90; the realigned 60%-achievable target is shown as a *scenario*. Baselines table: model error vs persistence and seasonal-naive.
4. **Corrective actions (40 s).** The LP compresses the overhaul, adds derated shifts, and reallocates ~1,500 t to Ukwa using its spare capacity. Approve one action; switch the browser offline, defer another, and show it queued and synced.
5. **What-if (20 s).** Heavy monsoon → deficit rises; the optimiser re-plans. Tagged SCENARIO.
6. **Integrity (20 s).** Live checklist all green; limitations stated up front. Switch to हिन्दी / मराठी.
